# STUDENT ROBOTICS 2026
This project was made as an entry into the Student Robotics 2026 competition, ‘The Neutral Zone’. In this competition, robots had to collect acidic and basic samples to change their laboratory’s pH. One acidic sample decreased their lab’s pH by 1, and one basic sample increased their lab’s pH by 1. Points were awarded based on the difference between the ending pH of the lab and its starting pH of 7.

I have attached our groups code; however, as vision engineer for the group I wrote Vision.py as my main job. I focused on how the robot would find and respond to the samples in its environment. 

Closer to the competition, I also wrote Helpers.py and Strategy files, alongside pair programming provisional Comp Code.
